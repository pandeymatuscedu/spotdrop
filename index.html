Product Management Case Study

SpotDrop
for Spotify

A proximity-based social music discovery feature that lets Spotify users see what people nearby are listening to in real time — and connect over shared taste without saying a word.

The Opportunity

A social layer
Spotify doesn't have.

Millions of people listen to music in public spaces every day — on trains, in cafes, at airports. Despite being surrounded by others, there is no way to discover what is playing on the headphones next to you. SpotDrop changes that.

🎧

The Problem

Spotify's discovery tools are algorithmic and impersonal. They optimize for your past behavior. They cannot tell you what the person three seats away is listening to. Music is consumed in complete isolation, even in densely shared physical spaces.

✦

The Solution

An opt-in proximity feed that shows nearby listeners in real time. Users can save a track instantly, send a non-verbal listen-together request, or browse anonymously. Privacy first. Frictionless by design. Native to Spotify.
